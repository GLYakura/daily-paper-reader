## 摘要
我们展示了四个先进ACTPol（AdvACT）多色探测器阵列的光谱响应测量结果，这些阵列在中心频率约为30、40、90、150和220 GHz的频带内进行观测。通带使用傅里叶变换光谱仪（FTS）结合耦合光学元件进行测量，这些光学元件将FTS的输出引导并匹配到AdvACT接收器。我们对FTS和耦合光学元件进行光学仿真，以建模该系统中与频率相关的系统效应。我们利用这些仿真对测量结果进行修正，并评估剩余的系统不确定性。我们将这些系统估计与统计误差相结合，以确定我们FTS测量得到的通带及其不确定性，通常每个探测器阵列为0.75%至1.5%。我们在一个统一框架下呈现ACT所有测量的通带特性。此外，我们还讨论了所实现的性能以及未来改进通带测量的方法。

## Abstract
We present measurements of the spectral response of the four Advanced ACTPol (AdvACT) multichroic detector arrays, which observed in frequency bands centered near 30, 40, 90, 150, and 220 GHz. The passbands were measured with a Fourier transform spectrometer (FTS) combined with coupling optics that direct and match the output of the FTS to the AdvACT receiver. We perform optical simulations of the FTS and coupling optics in order to model frequency-dependent systematic effects in this system. We use these simulations to apply corrections to the measurements and evaluate the remaining systematic uncertainty. We combine these systematic estimates with statistical errors to determine the measured passbands and their uncertainties from our FTS measurement, which are typically 0.75 to 1.5% per detector array. We present the passband characteristics for all measurements made by ACT in a common framework. Additionally, we discuss the performance achieved as well as ways to improve passband measurements in the future.
