## 摘要
超高能粒子（$\gtrsim10^{18}$ eV）的无线电探测依赖于宽带无线电脉冲如何从自然介质边界反射。我们将单个均匀界面的球面波（Sommerfeld--Weyl）处理扩展到分层介质，方法是将每个平面波分量的菲涅耳系数替换为分层介质的特征矩阵反射系数，并在球面表面的局部切平面上进行评估。当层对比度被移除时，计算以机器精度简化为单边界结果，并且在十个HiCal-2仰角上与已发表的球面表面计算符合度优于$1.1\%$，平均偏差为$0.6\%$。我们将该形式应用于浅层粒雪堆，这些堆被提议作为异常极性ANITA事件的解释。对于实际的粒雪对比度，分层会改变反射幅度，但在所研究的仰角下，不会在$150$--$850$ MHz范围内反转脉冲极性。在参考的$s$偏振双层模型中，系数符号变化需要在局部仰角$8^\circ$、$15^\circ$和$25^\circ$处分别具有埋藏折射率$n_2\simeq2.56$、$2.04$和$1.79$。我们还测试了快速传播模型中使用的镜面分解，发现对于高空气球几何结构，与全角度积分的符合度为$0.2\%$，而近边界源则需要全积分。计算出的反射脉冲在$106$个HiCal-1直接/反射脉冲对中复现了预期的极性反转（$101$个）。由于介质通过其复折射率进入模型，该框架适用于冰、月球风化层和导电介质。

## Abstract
Radio detection of ultra-high energy particles ($\gtrsim10^{18}$ eV) depends on how broadband radio pulses reflect from natural media boundaries. We extend the spherical-wave (Sommerfeld--Weyl) treatment of a single homogeneous interface to stratified media by replacing the Fresnel coefficient of each plane-wave component with the characteristic-matrix reflection coefficient of a layered medium, evaluated in the local tangent plane on the spherical surface. The calculation reduces to the single-boundary result at machine precision when the layer contrast is removed and agrees with the published spherical-surface calculation to better than $1.1\%$ at ten HiCal-2 elevation angles, with a mean deviation of $0.6\%$.   We apply the formalism to shallow firn stacks proposed as explanations for anomalous-polarity ANITA events. For realistic firn contrasts, layering changes the reflected amplitude but does not reverse the pulse polarity over $150$--$850$ MHz for the elevations studied. In a reference $s$-polarized two-layer model, a coefficient sign change requires buried refractive index $n_2\simeq2.56$, $2.04$, and $1.79$ at local elevations of $8^\circ$, $15^\circ$, and $25^\circ$, respectively. We also test the specular factorization used in fast propagation models, finding $0.2\%$ agreement with the full angular integral for high-altitude balloon geometries, while near-boundary sources require the full integral. The calculated reflected pulses reproduce the expected polarity inversion in $101$ of $106$ HiCal-1 direct/reflected pulse pairs. Because the medium enters through its complex refractive index, the framework applies to ice, lunar regolith, and conducting media.
