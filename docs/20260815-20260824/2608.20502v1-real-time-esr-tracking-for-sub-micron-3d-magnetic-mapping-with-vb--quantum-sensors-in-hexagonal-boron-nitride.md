## 摘要
六方氮化硼（hBN）中带负电的硼空位中心（$V^-_B$）的自旋相关发光特性的发现，为范德华材料量子传感提供了新平台。特别是，利用光探测磁共振（ODMR）确定hBN色心电子自旋共振（ESR）频率的可能性，成为亚微米分辨率磁场量子传感的有力工具。然而，由于ODMR对比度较低，目前用于直流磁场映射的技术需要数小时的积分才能获得微米级区域的磁图像。在本工作中，我们报告了一种频率跟踪方法的实现，用于实时监测hBN中局域$V^-_B$中心的ESR频率。利用该技术，磁场监测被用于在仅几分钟内映射微米级锥形磁尖产生的磁场分布。通过控制磁性样品相对于量子传感器的位置，实现了衍射极限分辨率和散粒噪声极限灵敏度（54 $\mu$T$/\sqrt{\text{Hz}}$）的三维磁场映射。我们系统测量到3.6 $\pm$ 0.2 $\mu$T/nm的磁场梯度，并实现了6 mT/s的最大检测场变化率。本研究的结果确立了自旋共振频率跟踪作为一种可行的技术，用于分钟级磁成像的快速方法，与传统技术相比，采集时间至少减少一个数量级。

## Abstract
The discovery of spin-dependent luminescent properties of negatively charged boron-vacancy centers ($V^-_B$) in hexagonal boron nitride (hBN) enabled a new platform for quantum sensing with van der Waals materials. Particularly, the possibility of performing optically detected magnetic resonance (ODMR) for determining the electron spin resonance (ESR) frequencies of hBN color centers became a strong tool for quantum sensing of magnetic fields with submicrometric resolution. However, due to low ODMR contrast, current techniques proposed for mapping DC magnetic fields require hours of integration to obtain a magnetic image of a micron-sized region. In this work, we report the implementation of a frequency-tracking approach for real-time monitoring of ESR frequencies of localized $V^-_B$ centers in hBN. With this technique, magnetic field monitoring was used to map the field pattern generated by a micron-sized conical magnetic tip in only a few minutes. By controlling the magnetic sample's position relative to the quantum sensor, three-dimensional magnetic mapping of the field was achieved with diffraction-limited resolution and shot-noise-limited sensitivity of 54 $μ$T$/\sqrt{\text{Hz}}$. Magnetic field gradients of 3.6 $\pm$ 0.2 $μ$T/nm were measured with our system, in which a maximum detected field rate of 6 mT/s was achieved. The results of this study establish spin resonance frequency-tracking as a viable technique and fast method for minute-scale magnetic imaging, reducing acquisition times by at least one order of magnitude if compared to conventional techniques.

## 2. 方法论
