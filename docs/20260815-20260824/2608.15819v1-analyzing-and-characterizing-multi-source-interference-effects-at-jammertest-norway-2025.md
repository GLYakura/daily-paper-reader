## 摘要
来自低成本GNSS干扰机的有意射频干扰日益威胁基于卫星定位的精度和可靠性。缓解这一威胁不仅需要检测，还需要稳健的波形分类与表征、用于定位的到达方向推断，以及在真实运行条件下对接收机性能影响的估计；所有这些都受到设备、传感器、环境和卫星几何之间强烈分布偏移的挑战。我们通过汇编2025年Jammertest（挪威安岛）期间记录的专用真实世界数据集来解决这些挑战，该数据集涵盖两个室外测试区域，并行测量来自单天线E1/E5接收模块和2x2 CRPA阵列的数据。数据集涵盖多种干扰、欺骗和诱骗场景，包括CW、PRN、扫频/线性调频和多发射器配置，并辅以每次记录的元数据，支持时间对齐的真实参考。在方法上，我们基准测试了17种机器学习（ML）架构，用于干扰调制识别和多任务表征（类型、占用带宽和信号强度），并通过接收机感知的频谱分离系数（SSC）量化导航相关的退化，该系数将测量频谱映射为有效(C_s/N_0)_{eff}损失。为实现多源分析，我们迁移了在带标签频谱图数据集上预训练的YOLOv8s和RF-DETR检测器，以在GNSS频谱图中定位多个同时存在的干扰源，并随后表征每个检测到的分量。结果显示区域内分类准确率接近99%，但区域间性能显著下降，突显了对真实世界域偏移鲁棒性的需求。

## 速览
**TLDR**：低成本GNSS干扰器威胁定位精度，需同时实现检测、分类与定位。基于Jammertest 2025挪威实测构建多场景干扰数据集，用17种ML模型做调制识别与多任务表征，并迁移YOLOv8s/RF-DETR定位多源干扰。域内分类准确率近99%，但跨测试区显著下降，凸显真实域偏移的严峻性。 \
**Motivation**：干扰威胁要求稳健的识别定位方法，但真实场景存在明显域偏移，需专用数据与基准支撑。 \
**Method**：构建双场地多干扰实测数据集，用17种ML架构识别调制类型并预测带宽/强度，再迁移检测器实现多源定位。 \
**Result**：域内分类近99%，跨区性能骤降；SSC系数有效关联干扰谱与载噪比损失。 \
**Conclusion**：现有模型对域偏移鲁棒性不足，该数据集与基准可推动实用化干扰对抗技术研发。

## Abstract
Intentional radio-frequency interference from low-cost GNSS jammers increasingly threatens the accuracy and reliability of satellite-based positioning. Mitigating this threat requires not only detection but also robust waveform classification and characterization, direction-of-arrival inference for localization, and impact estimation on receiver performance under realistic operating conditions; all of which are challenged by strong distribution shifts across devices, sensors, environments, and satellite geometries. We address these challenges by compiling a dedicated real-world dataset recorded during Jammertest 2025 (Andoya, Norway), covering two outdoor test areas with parallel measurements from a single-antenna E1/E5 receiver module and a 2x2 CRPA-array. The dataset spans diverse jamming, spoofing, and meaconing scenarios, including CW, PRN, sweep/chirp, and multi-emitter configurations, and is complemented by per-recording metadata enabling time-aligned ground truth. Methodologically, we benchmark 17 machine learning (ML) architectures for interference-modulation recognition and multi-task characterization (type, occupied bandwidth, and signal strength), and we quantify navigation-relevant degradation via a receiver-aware spectral separation coefficient (SSC) that maps measured spectra to effective (C_s/N_0)_{eff} loss. To enable multi-source analysis, we transfer a YOLOv8s and RF-DETR detector pretrained on a labeled spectrogram dataset to localize multiple simultaneous interferers in GNSS spectrograms and subsequently characterize each detected component. Results show near-99% within-area classification accuracy but substantial cross-area performance drops, highlighting the need for robustness to real-world domain shifts.
